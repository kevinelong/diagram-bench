## 2. What changed (all four in one commit, suite green)

1. **Nine formats instead of four.** `IMAGE_SUFFIXES` is now `png webp jpg jpeg gif bmp tif tiff avif` — exactly what
   Pillow 12.1 in the Comfy venv can decode. `jxl` and `heic` are deliberately absent because that Pillow cannot open
   them. Everything downstream (thumbnail, signature, size badge, base, upscaler) already went through PIL, so the
   tuple was the whole change.
2. **The strip is the whole gallery, never scoped to the open folder.** Scoping it hid every picture the grouping could
   not file: on the real gallery, the default-opened family held 844 of 2,160 and **83 unfiled pictures — seven pasted
   reference screenshots among them — were invisible in the strip and in every search**.
3. **A `no prompt` filter and a `no meta` corner badge.** A picture with no sidecar can never join a family, so it needs
   a way to be found and a way to look different on the shelf.
4. **One tile ladder, bounded caches, and a split that escalates.** Tiles snap down to 16 px rungs (10 sizes over a
   221-pixel sweep instead of 221), the ceiling is `THUMB_PX` 336 instead of 420 (420 was upscaling a thumbnail),
   the PhotoImage cache is LRU-bounded at 400, `read_meta` is memoised by `(sidecar, mtime)`, and a loose family whose
   largest piece stays over 60 % retries the split threshold twice before giving up — which took the real gallery from
   64 folders to **80** and the largest family from **844 to 456**.

`test_tiles.py` is new (28 assertions). Three assertions in `test_gallery_ui`/`test_sizing` encoded the old contracts
(strip scoped to the folder, tile size equal to the band value) and were rewritten next to the measurement that
reversed them. Sixteen suites pass.

## 3. What was found and NOT fixed (so the next session does not have to rediscover it)

- **An exotic base is the engine's problem, not the gallery's.** Arming a `.gif`/`.avif` as a base uploads it and asks
  ComfyUI's loader to decode it. ComfyUI's core is bundled inside the desktop app, so the loader's extension policy could
  not be read on disk; a failure would surface as the server's own error. Converting such a base to PNG before upload is
  the obvious next step **if** it is ever seen in practice — it was not tested tonight and is not claimed.
- **The 87 unfiled pictures have no prompts.** `Describe all` already exists for a folder; pointing it at the unfiled
  bucket would give them captions, after which they join families normally. Not done, not measured.
- **456 pictures in one family is correct** (the velvet-dress series is one scene with many versions) but it is worth
  re-measuring when the prompt series changes, because the escalation now stops at 0.50.
