regression is the open thread.

## Measured 2026-10-21: a numeric budget, and a matcher that was over-crediting labels

Same FLUX.1-schnell Q4_K_S arm, seed 1234, 4 steps, identical suffix and scorer; only the prompt text differs.
**The label matcher was wrong on the first pass** - `any(label in text)` credited `7` inside `70` and `120` inside
`1120`. Corrected to exact tokens and everything re-scored:

| prompt set | substring (retracted) | **exact token** | paths_median |
|---|---|---|---|
| frozen (one-liners) | 0.552 | **0.406** | 98 |
| grounded v1 (coordinates only) | 0.269 | **0.183** | 149 |
| grounded v2 (words + coordinates) | 0.437 | **0.337** | 104 |

Retracted on the correction: blueprint-1 v2 "2/2" is 0/2 (the reader saw `1120`), and tree-1 frozen "6/7" is
**1/7**. Ordering survives; two specific claims do not. **Every historical score in this repo used the substring
matcher**, so older `result-*.json` numbers are inflated wherever a required label is a short token or a digit.

**Density: the single-seed curve was noise.** Replicated with three seeds per rung (1234/5678/9012), exact token:

| numbers | 512 mean | 1024 mean |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | 2.67/4 | 1.67/4 |
| 10 | 2.67/4 | **3.33/4** |
| 18 | 2.00/4 | 2.67/4 |
| 34 | 2.67/4 | **1.33/4** |

At 512 there is **no trend** (34 numbers scored [3, 1, 4]). At 1024 the only surviving comparison is 34 < 10
(non-overlapping seed ranges), i.e. a top-rung penalty against the best rung, **not** a curve. The earlier "knee at
10, leakage at 18, collapse by 34" is **withdrawn**. What survives qualitatively at both sizes: at 18-34 numbers the
coordinates appear on the drawing as labels.

**Tree label binding is unsolved at every density.** frozen one-liner **1/7**, schema (51 numbers) 0/7, boundary (15
numbers) 1/7 with axis **100** - the render shows correct tree geometry whose nodes carry the prompt's own numbers
(`70`, `40`, `66`, `212`) instead of 1..7. So: density governs *structural* fidelity, and label binding fails independently of it. **Correction**: single-digit labels are unmeasurable *for schnell at 1024*, not for the task - Z-Image reads tree-1
at 12/14 there, and at **512 schnell itself reads 4/7**. Resolution is an uncontrolled moderator: every experiment
today ran at 1024, so the numeric budget below is a 1024 statement, not a general one.

## 2026-10-24: the base-image contract, and why a run uploaded an image with the box unticked

Reported symptom: the window sent an image to the model while *reuse last image as base* was unchecked. Cause, from the
code and from the sidecars in `Pictures/zimage-ui`: the old checkbox was read in one place only, *after* a run
(`zimage_ui.py`, the `chain_var` branch in `_poll`), where it promoted the finished image to base. What actually decides
an upload is `self.base_path`, and a **plain click in the strip** armed it as a side effect - `_on_tile_click` →
`select_file` → `_set_base` - so clicking a thumbnail to look at it, or to recover its prompt and seed, silently turned
every later Enter into an img2img at the saved strength (0.43 in that session). The session data shows the signature:
the 21:00:44 run carried base `...140529` **and that base's own seed 267546281**, which is `select_file` restoring a
seed, not a chained run.

Fixed by separating looking from editing:

- `select_file` no longer sets the base; it shows the picture and restores its prompt and seed, and the status line
  points at the new button.
- **Use as Base** (new, above Clear Base, enabled once a picture is shown) is the deliberate gesture: it arms the base,
  restores that image's prompt and seed, and says so.
- Ctrl+V still pastes *and* arms, because pasting is already an explicit "edit this".
- The checkbox is relabelled **chain: each new image becomes the next base**, and its tooltip no longer implies control
  over uploads.
- A run that uses a base now prints its age (`made 6h 51m ago`) in the hint line, so an old pick cannot be sent silently.
- The seed is kept through a run only while a base is armed; a click alone no longer pins it.

Verified: `test_gallery_ui.py` **310 PASS, 0 failures, exit 0** (drives the real window with a stubbed engine), with new
checks for "a click does not become the base", "Use as Base sets the base", "generate really passes it", "Clear Base
disarms it again", and "looking at an image does not pin its seed".

## 2026-10-25: which unfiltered klein is "stronger" - three arms on the frozen corpus

"Stronger" turned out to mean two different things, so both were measured rather than argued.

**Rung 1 - same 4B weights, better encoder.** `nerualdreming` q4_k_m (2.33 GiB) replaces the shipped fp4 encoder.
**Rung 2 - a bigger model.** `unsloth/FLUX.2-klein-9B-GGUF` Q4_K_M (5.50 GiB, ungated) with a 9B uncensored encoder
(`wy8edhuh` Q5_K_M, 5.45 GiB, ungated, a re-quant of ponpoke's Qwen3-8B). Both byte-verified against the API's own
sizes, and both read as what they claim: the diffusion file is `arch flux`, 201 tensors, `double_blocks.*`; the encoder
is `arch qwen3`, 36 blocks, tokenizer embedded.

`generic_arm.py` gained the two arms plus `--host` (the server serving this box is on 8006, not 8188). Frozen corpus,
512 px, seed 1234, 10 prompts, RapidOCR scoring - one implementation, no opinion:

| arm | OCR exact | prompts fully legible | paths | nodes | axis | median | peak VRAM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `klein` (4B, shipped fp4 encoder) | 0.613 | 1/10 | 50 | 811 | 100.0 | 14.3 s | 6,949 MiB |
| `klein_unc` (4B + uncensored q4_k_m) | 0.649 | 2/10 | 38 | 656 | 99.0 | 13.7 s | 6,949 MiB |
| `klein9_unc` (9B + uncensored Q5_K_M) | **0.732** | **4/10** | **28** | **460** | 100.0 | **32.5 s** | **8,837 MiB** |

Read it plainly: the 9B is the stronger model - **+19 % OCR exact over the 4B, four clean prompts against one, and it
traces into fewer, simpler paths** (28 vs 50) - at 2.3x the wall time and 1.9 GiB more VRAM, which still leaves 3.4 GiB
of the 12,227 MiB card. The visible difference is spelling: the 4B writes `VAILDATE`, the 9B writes `VALIDATE`.
Second finding, unexpected: the uncensored encoder also **helps** on neutral prompts over the shipped fp4 encoder
(+0.036 OCR exact, half the paths), so fp4 quantisation of the encoder was costing label fidelity. n=1 seed, 10 prompts -
direction, not significance.

Added as profile **`flux2 klein 9b uncensored`** (proven by the run above) beside `flux2 klein uncensored`.

Evidence: `result-klein-512-20261025.json`, `result-klein_unc-512.json`, `result-klein9_unc-512.json` and their
`-scores` files; renders under `render/<arm>-512/`. **An incident, recorded because it nearly cost evidence:** the arm
runner writes `result-<profile>-<size>.json` unconditionally, so re-running the `klein` baseline overwrote a 2026-10-21
measurement (idle VRAM 11,070 MiB, first wall 3.0 s). It was restored from git and today's run kept as the dated file;
`generic_arm.py` now **refuses to overwrite** an existing result unless `--force`, and has `--out-name` for a dated one.
