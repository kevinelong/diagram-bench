NOTE 2026-11-14 - test_sizing.py's B3 stall: what the stack says, and the test-only fix (0.30.1)

WHAT WAS ASKED
`python test_sizing.py` stalled in its B3 block ("the open-folder pane keeps its pictures inside the canvas"): 52
PASS lines and then nothing, at 200 s, 600 s and 1800 s, identically at 0.29.1 and 0.30.0 (recorded in
`notes/2026-11-13-test_sizing-stall-is-pre-existing.txt`). Diagnose the hang, then fix it or make it fail fast with a
named reason - without weakening an assertion and without deleting a block - and record whether the three
pre-existing failures of 2026-11-03 become reachable.

scrollregion `0 0 1 9184`, the scrollbar 1 px, and `contents_holder` reports **no geometry manager at all**

lay-out it just did does not match the metrics the 1 px canvas now gives it, and rebuilding again 60 ms later.

WHY THE PANE IS 1 PX: THE SCREEN, NOT THE DESKTOP
`contents_holder` has no manager because `set_view_mode` grid_removes it - by design - on every screen except
`columns` and `images` (`zimage_ui.py:5610`). A fresh profile opens on `view_mode = ... or 'folders'`
(`zimage_ui.py:2904`), and on the folders-only screen the pane, the prompt column, the picture column and two of the
three sashes are all removed on purpose. B3 then called `select_folder(0)` **on that screen**: a person cannot do that
- clicking a folder goes through `_drill_click`, which opens the folder *and* switches to the pictures screen - so the
block was opening a folder whose pane is not on screen, laying 96 cells into a 1 px canvas, and waiting for a window
that has nothing to settle. `_show_pane` does not rescue it: it re-grids the holder only for `images`/`columns`
(`zimage_ui.py:8268`), so on `folders` the rebuild is invisible, unmeasurable, and endless.

That is also why the stall is pre-existing at 0.29.1: the *test* never put the pane on screen, at either version, and
the app's default screen has been `folders` since the screens were introduced (2026-11-03).

THE FIX - TEST-ONLY, TWO PARTS
1. **B3 opens on a screen that has the pane**, the way a person does, and says so before it measures anything:

       app.set_view_mode('columns', persist=False)
       pump(root, app, 1.0)
       check('the pane is on screen, so what it lays out can be measured',
             app.contents_holder.winfo_manager() == 'grid' and app.contents_canvas.winfo_width() > 1,
             (app.view_mode, app.contents_holder.winfo_manager(), app.contents_canvas.winfo_width()))

   No assertion was weakened and no block was dropped: the ten checks B3 already had are unchanged and now run against
   a pane that is really there (canvas 618 px, bar 14 px, 2 columns of 295 px tiles).

2. **Every wait in the file is bounded.** `update()` cannot be bounded from outside, so the suite no longer calls it:
   `drain()` is the same loop with a budget (`dooneevent(DONT_WAIT|ALL_EVENTS)` until the queue is empty or
   `DRAIN_CAP = 4.0` s pass), `settle()` is one accounted-for drain, and `pump()` uses it - plus the 26 other places
   that called `root.update()`/`win.update()`/`app.canvas.update()` directly. A queue that has not emptied at the cap
   is reported once per block, as the *fact* rather than as a diagnosis (a long paint and a loop look the same from
   there, and the numbers say which it was):

       <block> blocked: the event queue did not empty within 4.1 s (98 events dispatched), so this step could not be
       measured to the end

   with a `block(s) could not be run to completion` summary beside the failure list at the end. `step()` (the block
   headings, which used to be `print('\n-- ...')`) supplies the block's name, so the message says *where*, not just
   *that*.

   The cap is 4 s and not 1.5 s because one *honest* paint is long: at `DRAIN_CAP = 1.5` a clean run (2026-11-14,
   75 s) reported `zoom blocked: ... within 1.5 s (139 events dispatched)` on the 2400x1600 zoom, whose own checks
   then passed anyway - slow, not looping. What the bound has to catch is the other case: the stall dispatched 42
   events in 1.5 s while its geometry did not move at all, and 98 in 4.1 s when re-measured.

WHAT AN APP-SIDE FIX WOULD BE, FOR WHOEVER OWNS zimage_ui.py
Not done here - this change is confined to `test_sizing.py` + `version.py` - but the loop is the app's, not the test's:
opening a folder while the open-folder pane is not on screen (`view_mode == 'folders'`) builds a page of cells into a
1 px canvas and reflows it for ever, because `_show_pane` only re-grids the holder on `images`/`columns`
(`zimage_ui.py:8256-8271`) and nothing else tells `_render_contents` that its pane is not on screen. Either
`select_folder` should refuse/defer when the pane is not mapped, or `_render_contents` should not page into a 0-1 px
canvas.

Two further notes for that owner, read rather than measured at this point: `_render_contents` calls
`contents_canvas.update_idletasks()` (a nested, unbounded Tk wait *inside* the app, which no test-side bound can
reach), and the pane's rebuild is scheduled from both a timer (`_contents_settle`, 60 ms) and idle
(`_idle(self._on_contents_resize)`), which is how one 1 px pane can keep the queue fed without anybody asking it to.
With the pane on screen neither is a problem on this box - five clean runs, no `blocked:` line - but the app can still
spin if that state is ever reached in a person's window.
