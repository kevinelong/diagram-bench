NOTE 2026-11-14 - the rank order is one function now: fitted strength, then the record (0.33.0)

WHAT WAS ASKED
`order: Wins` ranked on raw wins, and it ranked on raw wins in two places at once: `_sheet_paths` (the strip's folder
tile) spelled out `(-rec['wins'], rec['losses'], -t[0])` and `_render_contents` (the pane) spelled out
`(-wins, -losses)`. The two copies are what let the tile feature a picture the pane ranked 11th (reported 2026-10-31).
`pair_rank` already held the better method - `bt_strength`, a Bradley-Terry fit over the per-opponent tallies in the
sidecars - and nothing in the window called it. `rank_key`'s docstring claimed the win rate "protects against
participation bias", which it cannot: the rate was the *second* key.

WHAT CHANGED
- `pair_rank.order(paths, tie_break=None)` is the one answer to "what is the rank order": fitted strength first, then
  `rank_key` - most wins, fewest losses, then the name. `tie_break` is the caller's own newest-first pass (the tile's
  plan order, the pane's `row_stamp` pass, the strip's timeline), so the stable two-pass each surface documented stays
  meaningful instead of being overwritten by an alphabetical last key.
- `MIN_DECIDED_BOUTS = 3`: fewer than three decided bouts (wins + losses, not `bouts`, which counts ties) is no fit, so
  such a picture is ordered after the pictures that have one, by its record.
- Both hand-written keys are gone; the tile, the pane and the strip's flat list call `order`. `sort_by_strength` is now
  `order`; `sort_by_wins` stays as the raw-count order (it is what the strength-beats-count test compares against).
- `badge` -> `12W-1L (13)` / `1W (2)` / `''`, and the review window's hint speaks the same text. It used to say
  "(rank N)" while printing the *win count* as N; N is now the actual place in `order`.
- A recorded win (or an undo) drops `_rank_cache`: the strip's order is cached against the picture *set*, and a bout
  changes nobody's membership, so the strip would have kept the order from before the win while the pane and the tiles
  already showed the new one. The folder tile already had this defence (its `_heads` key carries a wins stamp).
- `rank_key` -> `(-wins, losses, name)`, docstring corrected to say what it does now and why the rate and then the
  mtime are not in it.
- Pairing untouched: `pair_review.pair_up` and `EXHAUSTIVE_MAX = 6` are exactly as they were.

WHAT THE FIXTURES SAID, MEASURED (HEAD's `pair_rank.py` in a scratch dir vs the new one)
- strength beats count (test 4: a beats b twice and c once, b beats c, d farms five wins over a picture that never
  wins). Old `sort_by_wins`: d, a, b, c, nobody. Old hand-written tile/pane key: d first. New `order`:
  a, d, b, nobody, c. The raw-count order puts the farmer first; the fit puts the head-to-head winner first.
- determinism (test 3: three pictures, equal records, both files stamped to one mtime). Old key, input as given:
  tie_c, tie_a, tie_b. Old key, input shuffled: tie_b, tie_c, tie_a - the old key was input-order-dependent because
  `-mtime` tied and `sorted` is stable. New: tie_a, tie_b, tie_c both times.
- pane against tile (test 2's ladder: c0 beats c1 beats c2 beats c3 beats c4, three wins each, so strength runs
  oldest-first while recency runs the other way and the three middle pictures tie on raw wins and losses). Old pane:
  c0, c3, c2, c1, c4. Old tile: c0, c3, c2, c1, c4. New, both: c0, c1, c2, c3, c4.

THE HONEST PART: TWO OF THE FIVE ARE GUARDS, NOT REGRESSIONS
The brief's test 1 - "a picture at 3W-0L must not outrank one at 12W-1L, and prove it red on the old key" - cannot go
red on a wins-first key: 12 > 3, so every old key put the 12W-1L picture first. Measured on the fixture (3W-0L whose
three wins are all against the picture that always loses, 12W-1L that beat it eleven times and the folder's best once):
old `sort_by_wins` = earn12, best, farm3, weak; old hand-written tile and pane keys = the same first two; new `order` =
best, earn12, farm3, weak. The requirement held before the change and holds after, so the test is a guard on it, and the
thing it really pins is the reason: the fit gives the farming 3W-0L no strength at all (0.0000 against 0.2301). A pure
rate-first key would have failed it, but no such key existed - that was the docstring's claim, and the docstring was
wrong. The pane/tile *agreement* check is a guard for the same reason: the old pane and the old tile agreed on the
ladder fixture, by resolving the same ties the same (recency) way; what they got wrong was the order, and that is the
check that goes red on the old keys.
The genuinely red-before tests are therefore 3 (shuffle), 4 (strength beats count), and the "not recency and not the
counts" check inside test 2. Test 5 (the badge) is red before by construction: the old formatter returned `1W` and
`12W 1L` for the two cases, so `1W (2)` and `12W-1L (13)` both fail against it.

WHAT WAS NOT TOUCHED
`pair_review.pair_up`, its similarity-greedy walk and `EXHAUSTIVE_MAX`. The web server's own `recordText` in
`web/app.js` is a separate formatter for a separate surface and still prints `nW nL`; nothing here asserts they match.
